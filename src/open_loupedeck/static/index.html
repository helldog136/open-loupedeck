OBS Studio

…

Switch scenes, mute sources and change volume from your keys.

Details

In OBS, open Tools, then WebSocket Server Settings, and enable the server. "Show Connect Info" gives you the password.

Home Assistant

…

Run lights, scripts and scenes, and show sensors or the weather on your keys.

Details

In Home Assistant, open your profile, then Security, then create a long-lived access token. Paste it here with your Home Assistant address.

Spotify

…

Play, pause and skip tracks. Needs Spotify Premium and an open Spotify app on any device.

Details

If connecting fails with "user not registered", create your own free Spotify app: open the developer dashboard, create an app, tick "Web API", add the redirect address shown below, then paste its Client ID here.

Open the Spotify developer dashboard

Twitch

…

Make clips, run ads, change chat modes and show whether you are live. Works with several accounts.

Details

Click Connect and approve on twitch.tv; there is nothing to copy. Each Twitch action runs once for every account listed here.
